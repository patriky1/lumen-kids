Questions from families

Short answers to the things parents ask most.

What is Lumen Kids?

A calm learning companion for children ages 4–8 that we’re building now. Today you can use the free Creative Studio to make printable activities for your child.

Is the Creative Studio really free?

Yes. Every printable is made right in your browser, with no account, no sign-up and no limits.

Do you collect my child’s data?

No. This website has no accounts, no analytics and no forms that send information anywhere. Names and topics you type stay on your device. See our privacy page for details.

Which ages is it for?

The app is designed for ages 4 to 8. In the Creative Studio, puzzles go from Pre-K all the way to high school, so older siblings can join in.

Will Lumen Kids use points or rewards?

No. We focus on curiosity and the satisfaction of figuring things out, not streaks, coins or prizes.

What devices will the app support?

We’re designing for tablets and the web first. The Creative Studio already works in any modern browser and prints on Letter-size paper.

How can I share feedback or ideas?

We’d love to hear from you. Open an issue on our GitHub project.
