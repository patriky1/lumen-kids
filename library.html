Stories, read together.

From old favorites to surprising discoveries, every book is a small adventure.

Browse the shelves.

Pick a book by its cover, or ask Lumi for a story about dragons, oceans, or anything else your child is curious about today.

Read along.

Hear each page read aloud while the words light up, then switch the voice off when your child is ready to read on their own.

Ask about the story.

Wondering what a word means, or why a character made a choice? Children can simply ask, and Lumi answers in a way that fits their age.
