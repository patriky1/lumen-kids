How can we help?

Quick answers about the Creative Studio and Lumen Kids.

What is Lumen Kids?

A calm learning companion for children ages 4–8 that we’re building now. Today you can use the free Creative Studio to make printable activities.

Is the Creative Studio free?

Yes. Every printable is made right in your browser, with no account, no sign-up and no limits.

Why didn’t my topic work?

Word searches and crosswords come with word lists for the suggested topics. For any other topic, type your own words separated by commas. For crosswords, add a clue after each word, like “whale: the biggest animal in the sea”.

How do I print a sheet?

After you create a sheet, choose Print. Your browser’s print window opens with the page sized for Letter paper. You can also download it as an SVG file.

Do you collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics or forms that send information. Names and topics you type stay on your device. Read the privacy page for details.

Which ages is it for?

The app is designed for ages 4 to 8. Studio puzzles go from Pre-K to high school.
